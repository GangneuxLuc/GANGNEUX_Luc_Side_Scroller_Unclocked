Off the clock is a 2D SideScroller in which you play as a thief who found an time travel equipment and uses it to steal across time. 

The goal is the found the "Truth parchment" which holds a power capable of restructure the world.

But it is protected by the TRO aka the "Time Regulation Office" to prevent malicious intents to be accomplished.

Get your hands on the parchment by solving puzzles, parkour and fighting. Game meant to be played on XBOX controller.


Video game made over 4 months as the 2nd semester project in ESMA Bordeaux JV1.
